MOBILITY FLOW · 20 MIN
FOAM ROLL · 5 MIN
Roll each area 45-60s. Pause on tender spots 10s.
1. Quads (front thigh, roll hip to knee)
2. IT Band (outer thigh, hip to knee)
3. Hamstrings (sit on roller, roll glute to knee)
4. Upper Back / Thoracic Spine (arms crossed, roll mid to upper back)
5. Lats (lie on side, roller under armpit, roll slowly)

DYNAMIC MOBILITY · 10 MIN
Move through each position slowly. Breathe into every stretch.
1. World's Greatest Stretch — 5 reps each side (hip flexor + thoracic rotation + hamstring)
2. 90/90 Hip Switches — 2 × 8 each side (hip internal/external rotation)
3. Cat-Cow + Thoracic Rotations — 2 min (spine mobility)
4. Shoulder Pass-Throughs (band or PVC) — 2 × 10 (shoulder ROM)
5. Deep Squat Hold — 2 × 30s (ankle + hip + thoracic mobility)

STATIC STRETCHING · 5 MIN
Hold each stretch 45-60s. Breathe deeply — don't bounce.
1. Pigeon Stretch — 60s each side (glutes + hip external rotation)
2. Couch Stretch — 60s each side (hip flexor + quad)
3. Chest Doorway Stretch — 45s each side (pec major/minor)
4. Dead Hang from Pull-up Bar — 2 × 30s (spinal decompression + lat stretch + grip)

WHAT & WHY

Kegels target the pubococcygeus (PC) muscle — the hammock of muscle running from pubic bone to tailbone. A strong pelvic floor improves:

  • Core stability & intra-abdominal pressure during heavy lifts
  • Bladder & bowel control
  • Sexual health & endurance
  • Posture support (works with deep core & diaphragm)
HOW TO DO IT
  • Find the muscle: Imagine stopping urine mid-flow — that squeeze is your PC muscle
  • Contract: Squeeze and lift the pelvic floor upward (don't push down)
  • Hold: Maintain the squeeze for the prescribed duration
  • Release: Fully relax for the same duration as the hold
  • Breathe: Keep breathing normally — don't hold your breath
  • Do NOT flex abs, glutes, or thighs — isolate the pelvic floor only
WHEN TO DO THEM

Kegels can be done anywhere, anytime — nobody can tell. Best times:

  • Morning: Right after waking up (lying in bed)
  • Commute: While sitting in traffic or on the bus
  • Desk work: Set a reminder every 2-3 hours
  • Before sleep: Final set while lying down

Aim for 3 sessions per day, spread throughout the day.
