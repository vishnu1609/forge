Weekly Rotation — Telangana Style

Kodi (Chicken) Styles

  • Mon/Thu: Kodi kura (tomato-onion masala)
  • Tue/Fri: Gongura kodi (sorrel leaf)
  • Wed: Nalla karam kodi (black pepper dry fry)
  • Sat: Karivepaku kodi (curry leaf dry fry)

Millets / Grains

  • Lunch: 1 jonna rotte or 1 sajja rotte (40g flour)
  • Dinner: Ragi sangati (40g flour) or jonna rotte
  • Pappu swap (cut phases): 3 days/week, 1 katori pappu instead of the lunch rotte
  • Weekend: 2 pesarattu instead of the lunch rotte

Kooralu — 150g per meal

  • Fibre stars: Munagakaya, chikkudukaya, thotakura
  • Daily: Bendakaya, dondakaya, beerakaya, sorakaya, vankaya, potlakaya
  • Pachadis (1 tbsp): Gongura, tomato, allam

Pandlu (Fruit) — 1 Serving

  • Best: Jamakaya / guava, 100g (5.4g fibre)
  • Alt: Battayi / pear, 150g (4.6g fibre)
  • Alt: Danimma / pomegranate, 100g (4g fibre)
  • Seasonal: Seethapalam / custard apple, 100g (4.4g fibre)

Quick Recipes — Tap to Expand

Kodi Kura
25 min~79g protein / 350g raw
  1. Heat 1 tsp (5g) oil. Splutter mustard + cumin, add 1 onion (~80g), fry golden.
  2. Add 1 tsp ginger-garlic paste + 2 slit green chillies, fry 1 min.
  3. Add 1 tomato (~70g), 1 tsp red chilli powder, 1/2 tsp turmeric, salt. Cook until oil separates.
  4. Add 350g chicken pieces (raw weight). Cover, cook 15 min on medium.
  5. Finish with fresh coriander.
No water needed — chicken releases its own juices.
Gongura Kodi
30 min~79g protein / 350g raw
  1. Wash 100g gongura leaves, remove stems, roughly chop.
  2. Heat 1 tsp (5g) oil, splutter mustard, add 1 onion (~80g) + 4-5 dry red chillies.
  3. Add gongura, sauté 5 min until it melts.
  4. Add 350g chicken, 1/2 tsp turmeric, salt. Mix well.
  5. Cover and cook 18-20 min. No tomato, no water — sourness comes from gongura.
Red-stemmed erra gongura gives the authentic Telangana tang.
Nalla Karam Kodi
20 min~79g protein / 350g raw
  1. Dry roast 10g black pepper + 4 dry red chillies + 1 tsp cumin. Grind coarse.
  2. Heat 1 tsp (5g) oil, add curry leaves + 1 onion (~80g), fry golden.
  3. Add 350g chicken (boneless cooks faster), 1/2 tsp turmeric, salt.
  4. Cook 10 min covered, then add the nalla karam powder.
  5. Dry fry on high 3-4 min until coated and slightly charred.
Dry prep — pepper heat builds slowly. Adjust chillies.
Karivepaku Kodi
25 min~79g protein / 350g raw
  1. Blend 30g curry leaves + 4 green chillies + 1 tsp cumin + 10g ginger into a paste.
  2. Heat 1 tsp (5g) oil, pop mustard, add 1 onion (~80g), fry golden.
  3. Add 350g chicken, 1/2 tsp turmeric, salt. Sear 3 min on high.
  4. Add curry leaf paste, cover, cook 15 min.
  5. Open lid, dry out on high 2-3 min. Garnish with fresh curry leaves.
Use fresh dark-green curry leaves for the best aroma.
Kodi Guddu Pulusu
20 min~38g protein / 6 eggs
  1. Hard boil 6 eggs, peel, make 2 slits each.
  2. Heat 1/2 tsp (2.5g) oil in a non-stick pan, add mustard + cumin + curry leaves + 1/2 onion (~40g).
  3. Add 1/2 tsp ginger-garlic paste, 1 tomato (~60g), 1 tsp chilli powder, 1/2 tsp turmeric, salt.
  4. Cook until mushy. Add 200ml water + 5g tamarind (marble-size).
  5. Simmer 5 min, add eggs, cook 5 more min.
Tamarind is what makes it pulusu. Pour over ragi sangati.
Ragi Sangati
15 min1 serve = 40g flour · 4.6g fibre
  1. Makes 2 serves: boil 200ml water with a pinch of salt.
  2. Slowly add 80g ragi flour, stirring non-stop with a wooden ladle.
  3. Keep stirring hard — no lumps. Lower the heat.
  4. Cover 5 min, stir again until it comes together as one mass.
  5. Wet hands, shape into 2 balls. Eat 1, fridge the other for tomorrow.
Heavy-bottomed pan. Reheat leftovers with a splash of water.
Gongura Pachadi
10 minbatch · 1 tbsp/day
  1. Heat 1 tsp oil, fry 4-5 dry red chillies + 2 garlic cloves (~6g) + 1 tsp urad dal until golden.
  2. Add 100g gongura leaves, sauté 3-4 min until wilted.
  3. Cool fully. Grind coarse with salt.
  4. Temper with mustard + curry leaves in 1/2 tsp oil.
Makes ~6 tbsp — lasts the week in the fridge.
Pesarattu
10 min + soak~12g protein / 2 dosas
  1. Soak 100g whole green moong overnight (makes ~4 dosas).
  2. Grind with 2 green chillies + 10g ginger + salt. Slightly coarse batter.
  3. No fermentation — use right away.
  4. Spread thin on a hot tawa, 1/4 tsp oil around the edges.
  5. Cook till crisp. Serve with allam pachadi.
Weekend swap for the lunch rotte (2 dosas ≈ same calories).

Non-Negotiables

◆Weigh chicken raw. 350g raw ≈ 79g protein. Cooked weight would overstate it by ~30%.
◆Measure oil with a spoon. Every free-poured tsp is 45 kcal. Lunch budget = 1 tsp for kodi + 1/2 tsp for koora.
◆Jonna/sajja rotte over annam in cut phases. Annam comes back from diet break onward.
◆Ragi sangati at dinner: 40g flour per serve, not a full pot.
◆Munagakaya or chikkudukaya koora 3x/week for the fibre floor.
◆3.5L+ water daily — non-negotiable for uric acid clearance at this protein level.
◆Lipid panel + uric acid every 3 months with 6 eggs/day and 350g+ chicken.
◆No madyam. Spikes uric acid, kills recovery.
